About

This site is meant to offer an accessible and user-friendly interface to access Hesychius' Greek lexicon. The text comes from Greek Wikisource , not cross-referenced with any printed editions. My thanks to the Wikisource editors, to the Perseus Digital Library, and the Logeion project.

Entries

Each entry is read as a <headword> line followed by its definition, with outer brackets removed, printed marks (* † [) kept in grey, and page headings dropped, and all text is Unicode-normalized with repeated spaces collapsed. Entries typed in Beta Code (Greek in Latin letters, such as QHRÍON) are converted to Greek by the TLG rules and flagged.
